BHM Action Sports Schedule Support

BHM Action Sports Schedule helps you browse the event schedule, search events, save favorites, and synchronize favorites with Apple Watch.

Troubleshooting

If favorites are not synchronizing as expected, try these steps:

  • Close and reopen the iPhone and Apple Watch apps.
  • Confirm Bluetooth and Wi-Fi are enabled.
  • Confirm the Watch is paired with the iPhone.
  • Allow a short delay for favorites synchronization.
  • Restart both apps if synchronization stops.
