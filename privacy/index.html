BHM Action Sports Schedule Privacy Policy

Effective date: August 1, 2026

Privacy summary

BHM Action Sports Schedule does not collect, transmit, sell, or share personal information. No account or sign-in is required to use the app.

Favorites are stored on the user’s devices. Favorites may synchronize directly between the paired iPhone and Apple Watch.
